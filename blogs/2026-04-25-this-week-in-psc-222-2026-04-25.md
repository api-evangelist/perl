<p>This meeting took place at the Perl Toolchain Summit 2026. All three of us met in person in Vienna and reviewed our release blocker status. Things are in the clear: no new blockers have come in since last meeting, and the previously identified ones are already resolved, with a single exception, namely the workflow for EOL notices in release announcements. Conveniently, Philippe, who just released 5.43.10, is also in attendance in Vienna, and has provided concrete feedback, so this is being addressed.</p>
