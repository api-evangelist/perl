Hai again, after a very productive three weeks I can announce the next major release of Graphics::Toolkit::Color (despite the rather small version number jump). In this post I explain what changed(+12 spaces, +1 method, +7 method args), why it is relevant and how I used LLMs to achieve that. A lot more space to explore ! The 12 new color spaces are: LinearRGB, CIERGB, Adobe 98 RGB, Apple RGB, Pro Photo RGB, Wide Gamut RGB (Adobe), Rec.709, Rec.2020, Display P3, Display P3 Linear, DCI P3 and DCI P3 Linear.
